Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

This is a personal tool built and used by one person, Jared Gantt, to manage his own email and messages. It has no other users, no sign-ups, and no accounts.

What it accesses

Using Google account authorization, it can:

  • Read, search, organize, draft and send mail in the owner's own Gmail account
  • Read the owner's own Google Contacts, to match names to addresses

It has no access to any other person's account, and it cannot permanently delete mail or change account settings such as filters, forwarding or send-as identities.

What happens to the data

Data retrieved from these services is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests. It stays on infrastructure the owner controls. It is not sold, shared, published, used for advertising, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used to train machine learning models.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently stops this tool from reaching the account.
